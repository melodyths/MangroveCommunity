Ecosystem Superpowers

Why Mangroves Matter

Growing right where the land meets the sea, mangrove forests perform three vital jobs that keep both people and ocean wildlife safe.

Coastal Protection

Dense tangles of mangrove prop roots act like a living seawall. They absorb wave energy, reduce storm surges, and stop coastal soil from washing away during typhoons and floods.

Reduces wave height by up to 66%

Marine Habitat

Underwater roots create a safe nursery maze where baby fish, shrimp, crabs, and seahorses hide from large predators, while herons and kingfishers nest in the branches above.

Home to 1,500+ plant & animal species

Carbon Storage

Known as “Blue Carbon” champions, mangroves pull carbon dioxide (CO₂) out of the atmosphere and lock it deep inside their waterlogged muddy soil for hundreds of years.

Stores up to 4× more carbon than rainforests

Did You Know?

Super Adaptation

Mangroves can survive in salty ocean water that would kill most other plants! Some species actually push excess salt out through tiny pores on their leaves, leaving visible salt crystals behind.
